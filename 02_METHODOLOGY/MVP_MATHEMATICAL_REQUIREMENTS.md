# MVP Mathematical Requirements

## 1. MVP Mathematical Scope

The Minimum Viable Product does not require every mathematical model described in the methodology documentation.

The MVP should focus on the following.

### Required Mathematics

#### Basic Mathematics

* Arithmetic.
* Percentages.
* Ratios.
* Growth rates.
* Weighted averages.

#### Financial Mathematics

* Revenue.
* Gross profit.
* EBITDA.
* Profit margins.
* Debt calculations.

#### Valuation Mathematics

* Revenue multiples.
* EBITDA multiples.
* Enterprise value.
* Equity value.

#### Risk Mathematics

* Weighted risk scoring.
* Basic risk adjustments.

#### Analytical Mathematics

* Business health score.
* Valuation confidence score.

## 6. Testing Requirements

Every mathematical function must have automated tests.

### Revenue Growth Test

Input:

* Previous Revenue = ₦100M
* Current Revenue = ₦120M

Expected Result: 20%

### EBITDA Multiple Test

Input:

* EBITDA = ₦20M
* Multiple = 3.5

Expected Result: ₦70M

### Equity Value Test

Input:

* Enterprise Value = ₦100M
* Debt = ₦20M
* Cash = ₦10M

Expected Result: ₦90M

The development team should maintain a valuation test suite containing:

* Normal cases.
* Edge cases.
* Invalid data.
* Negative values.
* Missing values.
* Extreme values.
