# 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the SME Business Valuation & Intelligence Platform (the "Platform", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information.

We are committed to protecting the privacy of the business, financial, and personal information entrusted to us.

This policy applies to users of the Platform, including business owners, advisors, analysts, investors, and institutions.

**Legal Review Required:** This policy must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before commercial launch, including review against the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the laws of any other jurisdiction where the Platform operates.

# 2. Who We Are

The Platform is operated by the company identified in its business registration and contractual documents (the "Company").

# 7. AI Processing

The Platform uses AI to explain and interpret structured analysis results.

For certain features, information may be sent to external AI providers.

We will make reasonable efforts to:

* Provide structured, relevant context to AI providers
* Avoid unnecessary sharing of information
* Configure providers to avoid training on customer data where available and contractually appropriate
* Disclose AI processing in this policy and in the product

Enterprise customers may require additional no-training, data-processing, regional-processing, or model-restriction commitments, which will be documented in the applicable agreement.

AI-generated content should be verified. We are not responsible for errors in AI-generated explanations.
