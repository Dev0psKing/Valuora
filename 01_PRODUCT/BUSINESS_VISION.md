# Business Vision

## Vision

### Long-Term Vision

To become the leading business valuation intelligence platform for small and medium-sized businesses in emerging markets.

The company aims to build a financial intelligence ecosystem that allows millions of business owners to understand, monitor, protect, and increase the value of their businesses.

The long-term vision is to make business valuation as accessible as checking a company's financial performance.

A business owner should eventually be able to log into the platform and see:

> Current Estimated Business Value
> ₦75,000,000

> Change Since Last Quarter
> +12%

> Primary Value Drivers
> Revenue growth, improved profitability, recurring customers.

> Primary Risks
> Customer concentration and owner dependency.

> Potential Value Improvement
> ₦95,000,000 - ₦110,000,000.

---

## Mission

To democratize access to professional business valuation intelligence by providing small and medium-sized businesses with affordable, understandable, data-driven tools for estimating and improving business value.

---

## Final Product Positioning

### Short Description

An intelligent business valuation platform that helps SMEs estimate, understand, monitor, and improve the value of their businesses.

### One-Line Pitch

> We help small and medium-sized business owners understand what their businesses are worth and discover how to increase their value.

### Investor Pitch

> Our platform democratizes access to business valuation intelligence for small and medium-sized businesses. Using financial modelling, industry benchmarks, risk assessment, and multiple valuation methodologies, we help business owners estimate what their businesses are worth, understand the factors driving their valuation, and identify opportunities to increase business value over time.

### Long-Term Vision Statement

> To become the leading business valuation and financial intelligence platform for small and medium-sized businesses across emerging markets.

### Core Differentiator

> We do not simply tell business owners what their company is worth. We help them understand why, and what they can do to build a more valuable business.

## Future Vision

The ultimate vision is not to build:

> A website that calculates business valuation.

The ultimate vision is to build:

> **The financial intelligence infrastructure for small and medium-sized businesses.**

A future business owner could use the platform throughout their company's lifecycle.

### Start

Understand financial health.

### Growth

Monitor business performance.

### Expansion

Understand financing requirements.

### Investment

Estimate company value.

### Improvement

Identify value creation opportunities.

### Sale

Prepare the business for acquisition.

### Exit

Understand a defensible valuation range.

The platform becomes the business owner's financial intelligence companion.
