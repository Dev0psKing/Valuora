# Go-To-Market Strategy

**Product:** SME Business Valuation & Intelligence Platform
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Pre-Launch Strategy

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines how the platform will acquire its first users, validate product-market fit, establish credibility, generate revenue, and eventually scale distribution.

---

# 2. Initial Market Strategy

The platform should not attempt to serve every SME immediately.

Initial focus:

> **Digitally reachable Nigerian SMEs and professionals who already care about business performance, investment, financing, growth or exit.**

Potential early segments:

* Founders
* SME owners
* Consultants
* Accountants
* Business advisors
* Startup/SME ecosystem professionals
* Business brokers
* Investors

---

# 3. Beachhead Market

Recommended initial beachhead:

> **SME owners who want to understand their business value or prepare for financing/investment.**

Why:

* Clear pain
* Financial information exists
* Value is understandable
* Valuation creates an obvious activation event
* Strong potential for referrals

# 6. Founder-Led Sales

Early customers should be acquired manually.

Process:

```text
Identify SME
      ↓
Research business
      ↓
Contact owner
      ↓
Understand problem
      ↓
Offer analysis
      ↓
Run valuation
      ↓
Deliver report
      ↓
Collect feedback
      ↓
Improve product
```

Do not attempt to automate sales before understanding customer behavior.

---

# 7. Outreach Positioning

Avoid:

> "I built an AI valuation app. Try it."

Better:

> "I'm building a tool that helps SME owners estimate business value and identify the financial/operational factors affecting it. I'm looking for a small group of businesses to test the early version and give feedback."

The goal is learning first.

---

# 21. Trust Strategy

Financial products require credibility.

Build trust through:

* Transparent methodology
* Clear assumptions
* Data provenance
* Professional documentation
* Case studies
* Expert review
* Methodology versioning
* Clear disclaimers

Avoid exaggerated claims.

Do not market:

> "100% accurate valuation."

# 26. Customer Interview Program

Every early customer should be interviewed.

Questions:

1. How do you currently estimate business value?
2. Why do you need the valuation?
3. What was difficult?
4. What did you trust?
5. What did you distrust?
6. What result surprised you?
7. Would you pay for this?
8. How much?
9. What feature would make this indispensable?

# 29. Go-To-Market Risks

### Risk 1

SME owners do not perceive valuation as urgent.

**Response:** Position around financing, investment, planning and growth.

### Risk 2

Owners distrust automated valuations.

**Response:** Transparency and professional validation.

### Risk 3

Financial data is difficult to obtain.

**Response:** Guided onboarding and financial-data assistance.

### Risk 4

Customer acquisition is expensive.

**Response:** Advisor partnerships and referrals.

### Risk 5

Users only need valuation once.

**Response:** Build recurring business health and value monitoring.

# 31. GTM Principle

> **Sell the problem before selling the software.**

The customer does not care that the platform uses sophisticated algorithms.

They care:

> "Can you help me understand and improve the value of my business?"
